Our Story

Proudly Serving the Danforth & Beyond

Camwood Mechanical was founded with a simple idea: that every family and business in Toronto deserves reliable, honest HVAC service they can actually afford. We started in the Danforth neighbourhood and have been growing our presence across the GTA ever since.

We understand the unique climate challenges of Ontario winters and sweltering summers. Our team of licensed technicians brings hands-on expertise to every job — whether it's a furnace swap in a century-old East Toronto semi-detached, or a full commercial HVAC installation.

We're not a call-centre-dispatched operation. When you call Camwood, you're talking to someone who knows your neighbourhood and will treat your home like their own.
