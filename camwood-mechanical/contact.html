Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can you respond to a service call?

For standard service requests, we typically schedule within 1–2 business days. Emergency breakdowns during business hours are prioritized — call us directly.

Are your quotes really free?

Yes. We'll assess your situation and provide a clear, written quote at no charge. No obligation to proceed.

What does the 10-year warranty cover?

Our 10-year warranty covers all products we install — including furnaces, AC units, and heat pumps. It covers parts and labour for qualifying failures. We'll explain the full terms when we quote your job.

Do you work on weekends?

We're available Saturdays from 9am–4pm for scheduled appointments. Contact us to confirm availability.

Do you service all brands?

Yes — our technicians are trained to work on all major HVAC brands including Carrier, Lennox, Trane, York, Goodman, Amana, and more.
